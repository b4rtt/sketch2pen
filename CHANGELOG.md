# Changelog

## 2.0.1 – 2026-10-07

pen.dev → Sketch fixes found on a shadcn/ui dashboard document:

- **Image fills without an image** (e.g. generated avatars) are drawn like pen.dev draws them – a checkerboard with a
  grey outline – instead of disappearing; the image can be replaced in Sketch.
- **Instances that bring their own children** (slot components such as tables, rows and cells) keep their content.
  Before, tables generated by pen.dev were empty in Sketch.
- **Layout**: a row that sizes to its content is as tall as its children that do not fill it (cards with
  `fill_container` stretch to it instead of growing it); `space_between` spreads the free room even when the `gap`
  does not fit.
- **Text**: pair kerning from all of a font's kern lookups (Inter splits its kerning over several), so text widths match
  pen.dev in more cases (289 of 292 calibration texts on the pixel, was 273).
- `line` nodes are drawn.

## 2.0.0 – 2026-10-07

Both directions are complete: Sketch → pen.dev, pen.dev → Sketch, and a macOS app for both.

- **pen.dev → Sketch.** `sketch2pen Design.pen` writes `Design (Sketch).sketch`: frames and Sketch stacks with the
  positions pen.dev computes, shapes, paths, gradients, strokes (also per side), shadows and blurs, text on the same
  baselines, components as symbols with overrides and swaps (instances Sketch could not draw identically are
  expanded), colour variables as swatches, themes, icons from the icon fonts pen.dev uses, and the fonts and images
  the document needs embedded in the Sketch file. Google Fonts are embedded at the axis values pen.dev draws them
  with. Documents created by sketch2pen get their pages back. Verified with a new feature lab (`bun run lab:pen`,
  median 0.05 % of pixels differ) and real documents (`bun run compare:pen`).
- **Sketch ⇄ Pen app.** A SwiftUI app replaces the AppleScript droplet: pick or drop a `.sketch` or `.pen` file,
  switch the direction, set the options and convert; the window shows the report. English and Czech. The release
  archive is now `Sketch-Pen-macos.zip`.
- The CLI picks the direction from the file extension; new options `--no-symbols`, `--no-stacks`, `--offline` for
  pen.dev → Sketch.

## 1.1.0 – 2026-09-29

- **Underline and strikethrough are drawn.** pen.dev keeps them on the text without drawing them, so they become thin
  shapes placed exactly where Sketch draws them (measured with CoreText, per wrapped line, in the text colour).
- **Masks**: symbol instances used as masks (e.g. a circle symbol clipping a flag) clip with their symbol's shape;
  rectangles edited in Sketch that are still rectangles clip exactly with their corner radii; ellipse masks cut shapes
  exactly. Texts and instances inside path masks are only reported when they actually reach past the outline.
- **Fonts**: named instances of variable fonts (e.g. `SFPro-Semibold`) are found by their PostScript names – before,
  such fonts were substituted. Apple's SF Pro / SF Compact use the installed Text or Display cut matching the size, and
  shipped variable fonts get the letter spacing that keeps Sketch's text widths.
- Report: the text-decoration and path-mask notes only appear where something could not be reproduced.

## 1.0.0 – 2026-09-29

First public release.

- Converts Sketch documents (Sketch 43 – 2026 formats) directly into pen.dev `.pen` documents.
- Symbols → components with overrides, symbol swaps, tints, resized and scaled instances.
- Sketch Stacks → flex layout, verified against Sketch's saved positions.
- Exact emulation of what pen.dev lacks: inner shadows, shadow spread, several border widths, dashed borders,
  line-end markers, alpha masks (baked into images), path-shaped masks, paragraph spacing, mixed-style text,
  smooth corners (`--smooth-corners`).
- Fonts pen.dev cannot load are shipped with the document (from the Sketch file or the computer) or substituted with a
  metric-compatible Google font; `--font-map` overrides.
- CoreText helper on macOS: Sketch-exact line breaks and SF Symbols as vector paths.
- macOS drag-and-drop app, standalone CLI for macOS, Linux and Windows, HTML and JSON conversion reports.
